## Context

Many personal-site templates run, but are hard to evolve. This starter focuses on maintainable bilingual routing, content schemas, and deployment.

## What it includes

- unified content model for `posts` and `projects`
- `/zh` and `/en` route architecture
- automated GitHub Pages publishing plus search indexing

## Ideal use case

Great for long-term technical writing and project showcases.
