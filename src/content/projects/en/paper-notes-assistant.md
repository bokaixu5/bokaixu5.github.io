## Context

Most research teams read far more than they execute. This project focuses on converting reading artifacts into concrete experiments.

## Key capabilities

- extract testable hypotheses from long-form notes
- generate evaluation metrics and experiment steps
- export execution-ready task templates

## Status

Used in my personal research workflow. Next step is stronger multi-model comparison and failure playback.
