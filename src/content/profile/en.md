## Academic Overview

I have **27 papers accepted or published** in leading journals and conferences, including **7 first-author papers** and **3 corresponding-author papers**, with **over 300 citations**.
My publications appear in venues including **IEEE JSAC, IEEE TWC, and AAAI**.
As first author, I received the **Best Paper Award at the 2023 IEEE International Conference on Communications (ICC)**.
My other honors include the **2023 ZTE Outstanding Industry-Academia-Research Collaboration Project Award**, the **Outstanding Graduate Student Award at the 2025 International Conference on Ubiquitous Communication (Ucom)**, **Second Prize in the North China Regional Division of the 2026 Huawei Wireless Algorithm Competition**, **First Prize in the data algorithm competition at the 2026 IEEE International Symposium on Information Theory (ISIT)**, and the **"6G Xingchen" doctoral honor at the 2026 Global 6G Technology and Industry Ecology Conference**.
I was also **selected for the 2026 doctoral student special program of the China Association for Science and Technology (CAST) Young Science and Technology Talent Development Program**.
I have led **Huawei Spark Award projects**.
My research contributions were included in the **6G Near-Field Technology White Paper**, the world's first white paper dedicated to near-field technology, and I submitted **two standards proposals to the IMT-2030 (6G) Promotion Group**.
My research and academic achievements received official coverage from **People's Daily** and **China Education Daily** in **September 2026**.
